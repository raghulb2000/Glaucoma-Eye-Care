ABOUT GLAUCOVISION

Intelligent technology for retinal screening.

GlaucoVision is an academic deep-learning system designed to analyze retinal fundus photographs and classify images into glaucoma or normal categories.

The project demonstrates how artificial intelligence and medical image analysis can be combined to assist screening workflows.
